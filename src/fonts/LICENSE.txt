Bundled here rather than linked from Google Fonts because this app has to work
with the radio off. These are the `latin` and `latin-ext` subsets of the
variable font (weight axis 100-900), italic omitted since the app never uses it.

Inter replaced Inclusive Sans in v1.2. The two files that were here for it are
gone; the reason they are worth remembering is that Inclusive Sans shipped no
`tnum` feature, which is what the synthetic digit-advance hack in styles.css
existed to work around. Inter has real tabular figures, so that hack came out
with it. See `.tnum` in src/styles.css.
