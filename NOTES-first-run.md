# First run — answer tonight

Perishable. There is one window where this app can be seen with fresh eyes, and
it closes within about a week. Every rough edge normalises and then cannot be
found again. None of these answers can be reconstructed later.

**Rules for answering.**

- Twenty minutes, one sitting. Short answers. Fragments are fine.
- **Do not write fixes.** The moment a fix appears, the observation stops. If a
  fix insists on being written, put it in brackets at the end of the line and
  keep going.
- "Nothing" is a real answer and worth writing down — it is evidence the
  prediction held.
- Your words, not tidy ones. This file is raw material; it gets cleaned up when
  it gets promoted into Findings in NOTES-friction.md, not now.

Mark each answer with a tag where it fits: `LOOKS` `WORKS` `MISSING` `KEEP`.

**If you only do six, do the six marked ★.**

## 1. The original six

From NOTES-friction.md, unchanged.

1. Opening it for the first time, what did you go looking for that wasn't there?
2. What did you tap that turned out not to be tappable?
3. ★ Where did you hesitate, even for a second?
4. Would you have known to look in the Share sheet to install it, if you hadn't
   already known? (This is the first-run experience for every person you ever

5. What did you expect to happen after saving something, that didn't?
6. Anything you had to look at twice to understand.

## 2. What didn't happen

The most important section, and the one the original list misses entirely. The
app never sees these — there is no event for "gave up", so if it isn't written
here it does not exist.

7. ★ **What did you eat and not log?** Every instance. What was it about that
   moment — the food, the place, your hands, the time?
8. What did you log **later, from memory**, rather than at the time? How much
   later, and how confident are you the numbers are right?
9. Did you log anything **approximately** — grabbing a close-enough food, rounding
   a serving, picking a generic instead of the real product? What made the exact
   version not worth it?
10. Was there a moment you decided to open the app and then didn't? What stopped
    you between the decision and the icon?

## 3. Trust

A tracker that can't be trusted is worse than no tracker, and this class of
problem is invisible in tap counts.

11. ★ Did you ever look at a number and **not believe it**? Which number, and
    what did you do next — check it, ignore it, or re-log?
12. Did you find anything logged **wrong** after the fact? Wrong food, wrong
    amount, wrong day, wrong block. How long before you noticed, and how did you
    notice?
13. When you fixed it, did the fix feel safe — did you know what it was going to
    change before you tapped?
14. Is there anywhere you're **not sure** what the app is showing you? A number
    you use without being able to say exactly what it counts.

## 4. Hands and moment

Context is the variable that never survives to the write-up. Capture it while it
is still specific.

15. For each time you logged today: **one hand or two? Standing, sitting,
    walking? Phone already open, or from lock?** Roughly how long did the whole
    thing take, start to done?
16. Where did your thumb naturally land when the app opened, before you'd decided
    anything?
17. Did you ever have to change your grip to reach something?
18. Did you log anything **in front of another person**? Did the app take longer
    than the social moment allowed?

## 5. The thesis, tested

The Phase 0 candidate is *nothing here judges you*. Today is the only day you can
answer this honestly, before you know what the app is supposed to make you feel.

19. ★ If you went over on anything — **did it read as information or as
    failure?** First reaction, not considered opinion.
20. Was there a moment the app made you feel watched, or graded?
21. Was there a moment it felt **incurious** — where you wanted it to notice
    something and it just sat there?
22. Did you feel like you were feeding a database, or keeping a record? Those are
    different, and the second one is the thesis.

## 6. The predictions, checked

Day 0 in NOTES-friction.md made specific calls. Today is the first evidence.

23. ★ Did **Favourites and Recents** cover it, or did you end up searching?
    Roughly what fraction went each way?
24. Did you hit the **wrong target on a Recents row** — logging when you meant to
    adjust, or the reverse? How many times?
25. Did anything land in the **wrong block**, because the 12:00 / 17:00
    thresholds didn't match when you actually ate?
26. Did you want a **remaining** number — "how much protein is left" — anywhere
    the app only gave you `2504 / 2837`? What time of day?
27. Did you use the **plate**? If not, was it that you didn't need it or that you
    forgot it was there?
28. Did you reach for a control on a Favourites/Recents row **that wasn't there**?
    (The pencil became `+`; adjusting is now long-press or via the plate. This is
    a trade made on purpose and it is being watched.)
29. Did you search Open Food Facts? Did it feel **reliable**? Did you scan a
    barcode — in what light, and how many tries?

## 7. Seams

Things that betray it as a web app rather than an app.

30. Did it ever feel like a **website** instead of an app? What exactly gave it
    away — a scroll, a flash, a tap that felt slow, text that selected?
31. Did anything about **onboarding** ask you a question you didn't know the
    answer to, or that you had to guess at?
32. Second open of the day is a different app from the first — empty screen
    versus a screen with your data on it. **Was the second open better or worse?**

## 8. Keep

A log of only annoyances loses the evidence of what to protect, and the
protecting is what makes the redesign a design decision rather than a rewrite.

33. ★ What worked so well you didn't notice it until asked?
34. What would you be **annoyed to lose** in the changes you're about to make?
35. Is there one moment today you'd put in the portfolio as a five-second video?
