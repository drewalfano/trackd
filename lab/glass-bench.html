What to look for

Set Stripes, push Refraction to 60. If C looks identical to A, your browser is silently dropping the SVG filter — that is the Safari result, and it is the whole argument.

E is the escape hatch. It refracts by sampling the backdrop canvas directly, so it works everywhere WebGL does — but only over a backdrop you render yourself. Drag it over another specimen and watch it go blind.

Set Food log, then read the numbers. That is the shipping question, not the sparkle.
